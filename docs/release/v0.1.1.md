# v0.1.1: Replays, Flares, Chaff and Quality of Life

**Released:** 2026-09-26 · **Channel:** Pre-release · **Tag:** [v0.1.1](https://github.com/john-overton/T.O.R.E-Fighters/releases/tag/v0.1.1)

The first update to the [v0.1.0](v0.1.0.md) pre-release. The biggest addition is
a **flight recorder**: every flight now records itself, and you can watch it back
from any aircraft. Chaff and flares are in, with their original keys and sounds,
and the default keyboard now follows the original game's layout. There is also a
32-bit Windows installer.

> **Still an early pre-release, and the builds are still unsigned.** The install
> steps from [v0.1.0](v0.1.0.md#getting-started) have not changed.

## Highlights

### Mission replays

A T.O.R.E addition: the original's Replay Last Mission flew the mission again,
while this records what happened so you can watch it.

- **Every flight records itself.** Press **Ctrl+B** in flight to bookmark a
  moment ("Bookmark N saved") when something odd happens.
- **Replays** on the main menu lists your recordings newest first, with details,
  **Watch**, **Keep** and **Delete**. Old recordings are cleaned up for you: by
  default the last 20 are kept, plus any you mark Keep.
- The viewer plays forwards or backwards from 1/8x to 16x. Watch from every
  flight view on any aircraft or a free drone camera, with trails, labels, a
  timer, the Comms list, subtitles, a hide-UI mode and PNG screenshots. Sound
  plays at normal speed.
- Recorded chaff, flares and afterburner light play back as they happened.
- Debug panels show the AI's reasoning, flight-model telemetry, missile guidance
  and every radio call, with why each one happened. Right-click opens them in
  the viewer, and in live flight when **Pref > Debug panels?** is on.
- Recordings export to a **Tacview** file, a plain-English mission summary and a
  detailed debug log. These help trace AI behaviour, flight model quirks and
  radio bugs.

See [mission replays](../REPLAYS.md) for the full guide.

### Chaff and flares

- **Insert** releases one chaff cartridge and **Delete** one flare, as in the
  original. Keypad 0 and keypad "." do the same. On a gamepad, hold **View** and
  press D-pad left or right.
- You get the original messages, such as "Chaff launched, 3 left" and "Out of
  chaff". Unlimited ammo keeps the count full.
- A release can decoy a missile aimed at you, using the same odds the AI already
  uses.
- Each release plays the original chaff or flare sound. You hear other aircraft's
  releases at full volume within 100 ft, fading to silence at 4,000 ft.
- Flares leave in pairs and burn for 30 seconds. They light nearby aircraft,
  terrain, water and clouds, out to 1,500 ft and brighter at night. Chaff is a
  cloud of tumbling foil strips that drifts down over 20 seconds and flashes in
  the sun.

## Improvements

### Retail keyboard layout

The default keys now match the original game. The
[controls list](../CONTROLS.md) has every default binding.

- **1** to **5** set throttle from idle to 100%, **6** is afterburner, and **7**
  and **8** step 5%. Pressing 8 past 100% lights the burner, as in the original.
- **End** and **Page Down** are rudder, **O** opens the bay doors, **Shift+K**
  jettisons, **;** clears the target, **N** is NAV mode, and **W** and
  **Shift+W** step through waypoints.
- Keypad 5 centers the view, and **Shift+C** is slow motion again.
- Wing orders: **Alt+1** fly straight, **Alt+2** to **Alt+5** break, **Alt+6**
  to **Alt+9** approach, **Alt+R** engage from formation, **Alt+B** bug out,
  **Alt+T** next formation, **Alt+H** spacing and **Alt+V** stacking. Talking to
  one wingman moved to **Alt+Shift+1** to **Alt+Shift+4**.
- T.O.R.E-only controls moved to keys the original leaves free. The tower's
  next-airport key is now **Shift+N**, and repeat and cancel are
  **Ctrl+Shift+R** and **Ctrl+Shift+C**.
- Original keys for features that are not built yet, such as IFF and air-to-ground
  radar, now say "not implemented yet".

The [keyboard map](https://john-overton.github.io/T.O.R.E-Fighters/tore-keyboard-map.html)
has a new Replay sheet.

### Flight and presentation

- In-flight messages now appear at the bottom of the screen, like the original.
- Afterburner flames light up the scene around them, and keep their bright
  daylight colors at night.

### First-run import

- "Starting import..." appears the moment you click, with the current step in
  plain words underneath.
- The progress bar moves while the game files are still being identified, so the
  screen never looks stuck before the real progress count begins.

### Windows 32-bit installer

- A new **32-bit MSI** sits beside the 64-bit one on the
  [release page](https://github.com/john-overton/T.O.R.E-Fighters/releases/tag/v0.1.1).
  It installs under Program Files (x86).
- It needs no Visual C++ Redistributable.
- Installing either MSI replaces the other.
- The [download page](https://john-overton.github.io/T.O.R.E-Fighters/#downloads)
  links to it when a release carries one.

## Known limitations

- **The Tacview export has not been checked in Tacview yet.** It was built to
  Tacview's published file format.
- **The 32-bit MSI has not been tested by hand.** It is built on the release
  runners, and every crate lints and tests clean for 32-bit Windows in CI. It
  needs Windows 10 or later: the Rust toolchain does not support Windows 7, XP or
  older.
- Everything listed under [v0.1.0 known limitations](v0.1.0.md#known-limitations)
  still applies.
