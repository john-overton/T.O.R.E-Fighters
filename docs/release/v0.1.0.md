# v0.1.0: Quick Mission Creator

**Released:** 2026-09-24 · **Channel:** Pre-release · **Tag:** [v0.1.0](https://github.com/john-overton/T.O.R.E-Fighters/releases/tag/v0.1.0)

The first public build of **T.O.R.E-Fighters**, a ground-up Rust rebuild of
Jane's Fighters Anthology. This release wraps up most of **Milestone 1: Faithful
Quick Fight**. You can go from the original Choose Activity menu through Quick
Mission setup, fly and fight against AI wings over the original theaters, and
land at the debrief.

T.O.R.E stands for *Tasteful Opinionated Reverse Engineered*. We are rebuilding
what you experienced as a player, not the original program's code. **Bring your
own retail copy.** This release contains no retail game data, and the original
executable is never run.

> **This is an early pre-release, and the builds are unsigned.** Expect rough
> edges, and please report what you find.

## Getting started

1. Download the package for your platform from the
   [release page](https://github.com/john-overton/T.O.R.E-Fighters/releases/tag/v0.1.0).
2. Launch it and point it at your Fighters Anthology: an installed game folder or
   a mounted **disc 1**. You can drag the folder onto the window, pick a detected
   source, or type a path.
3. The game imports your media once, then opens at the main menu. No terminal
   needed.

| Platform | Package |
| --- | --- |
| Windows (x86_64) | `.msi` installer. SmartScreen will warn you: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. |
| macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) | `.dmg`. On first launch, right-click the app and choose **Open**. If macOS still refuses, use **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway**. |
| Linux (x86_64) | `.AppImage`, or `.tar.gz` |

You can re-import your media at any time from **Pref > Re-import media**.

## Highlights

### Menus and presentation

- The original **Choose Activity** menu, **Quick Mission** creator, **Load
  Ordnance** screen and **mission debrief**, all drawn with your own copy's
  artwork, fonts, animated rockers and sounds.
- The original recorded menu and briefing music. No MIDI synthesis.
- A graphics options screen with MSAA and render scale, plus a sharp rim that
  helps you spot distant aircraft.

### Aircraft and theaters

- **13 flyable aircraft:** F/A-18D, F-14D, A-4E, X-31 EFM, Rafale C, MiG-29,
  Su-27, MiG-21, Su-25, MiG-23, Su-35, F-22A and F-22N.
- **All 16 base theaters and 59 retail map variants**, with the original terrain
  heights, artwork, scenery, weather and day and night palettes.
- **Airports on every base theater**, with runway surfaces, individually
  targetable hangars and towers, tower radio and ILS guidance.

### Flight

- A fixed 120 Hz simulation, independent of your frame rate.
- Cockpits and HUDs that adapt to your window's aspect ratio, instrument windows,
  and the retail view suite with function-key controls.
- Working gear, flaps, airbrakes, hook, afterburner, stalls and spins, and runway
  wind.
- Heading, altitude and waypoint autopilot.
- **Ground starts** for your whole wing: taxi, take off, land and roll out.
- Ejection and pilot survival.

### Combat

- Guns with visible tracers, and 25 flight-ready weapons with four missile
  guidance types, pitbull activation, seeker search and HUD and tone feedback.
- A shared radar and infrared sensor model for all 13 aircraft: TWS and RWS,
  RWR, directional jamming and a radar cross-section page.
- Separate visual (Enter) and radar (T) targeting, a target camera, damage,
  smoke, debris and explosions.
- Blackout, redout and high-G screen shake.

### AI

- **Up to six wings and 29 AI aircraft** in one Quick Mission. Each aircraft has
  its own sensors, stores, fuel and flight model.
- **Four skill levels, Novice to Ace**, which change tactical choices, reaction
  time and countermeasure use.
- Visual search and memory, missile defense (jink, notch, dive), and escort and
  protect roles.
- **Wing orders**, delta formations, Bug Out, and Land at the selected airport.
- AI takeoff and landing queues that give you landing priority, and fuel-aware
  return to base.

### Sound

- Radio chatter and wingman replies from the original recordings, with Alt+S for
  radio silence.
- A cockpit crew voice. Two-seat aircraft get a RIO or co-pilot who coaches you
  through the fight.
- **In-flight music chosen by the original's situation rules.** Combat, launch,
  eject, success and almost-home scores all play at the right moments.
- Engine, actuator and environment audio, with spatial positioning.

### Cheats

- All of the in-flight Cheat menu except Damage Realistic: unlimited fuel and
  ammo, invulnerability, easy aiming and targeting, no spins, no crashes, no
  blackout, Enemy AI skill, guns only and more. Every cheat can be toggled
  mid-flight and survives Restart.
- The loadout screen's **Cheat** button.

### Controls

- A single, shared **controls screen** for remapping the keyboard, gamepads,
  sticks, throttles, pedals and button boxes, plus rumble.
- Head tracking through opentrack or TrackIR on UDP port 4242.
- An [interactive keyboard map](https://john-overton.github.io/T.O.R.E-Fighters/tore-keyboard-map.html)
  that you can export as a PNG or PDF.

### Under the hood

- Startup logs and crash reports on every platform. On Windows, startup errors
  also appear in Event Viewer.
- Linux, Windows and macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) are built and checked in CI
  on every change.

## Known limitations

- **Surface AI is not in yet.** SAM sites, AAA, vehicles and ships are not active
  opponents, and non-fighter aircraft families are not supported.
- **Only weapons that work in flight are listed.** The wider imported catalog
  comes in later weapon passes.
- **There are no campaigns, missions or multiplayer yet.** Quick Mission is the
  game mode in this release.
- **Some behaviour is fitted.** Where the original's exact behaviour has not been
  recovered yet, we use documented approximations labelled *fitted*. The
  [feature matrix](../features.md) lists what is complete and what is partial.
- **Windows and macOS have had less hands-on testing than Linux.** The packages
  build and pass their checks in CI, but real-world install reports are very
  welcome.
- **Carrier operations and waypoint routes are not implemented yet.**
