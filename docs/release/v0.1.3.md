# v0.1.3: Bug Bash, Overspeed, World Edge and Loaded Takeoffs

**Released:** 2026-09-29 · **Channel:** Pre-release · **Tag:** [v0.1.3](https://github.com/john-overton/T.O.R.E-Fighters/releases/tag/v0.1.3)

The third update to the [v0.1.1](v0.1.1.md) pre-release, and the result of the
first overnight bug battery: over 5,000 automated scenarios covering the menus,
every aircraft from ground start to landing, AI fights in all 16 theaters, and
mission recording. It fixes the bugs that battery found, including stores that
came back after you took them off, a crash with flares in the air, and AI pilots
flying into hills. Aircraft now need more speed to fly when they are heavy, can
be torn apart by overspeed, and are lost if they leave the theater. Load up a
Hornet with full fuel and feel the longer takeoff run.

## Highlights

### Heavier aircraft need more speed

- **Stall and liftoff speeds now rise with weight.** Fuel, stores and ordnance
  all count. Liftoff speeds go up by 17% to 40%: an F/A-18D with its default
  loadout now lifts off at about 147 knots, up from 112. Takeoff runs are longer
  to match.
- Combat turning is unchanged. At fighting speeds every aircraft pulls the same
  G as before, and weight only costs G below about twice the stall speed.
- The stall warning, autopilot, flight envelope window and crew callouts all use
  the new speeds.
- This is a T.O.R.E choice, not something shown in the original, made because
  real fighters need markedly more speed when loaded. Start the game with
  `--retail-stall-speeds` to fly the original's speeds.

### Overspeed, the world edge and belly landings

- **Overspeed.** From 95% of your top speed at the current altitude the cockpit
  shakes, growing to a clear shake at 100%, where "OVERSPEED" repeats every 4
  seconds. At 1.5 times the top speed the airframe fails. The debrief shows the
  cause. The Invulnerable cheat spares you the failure but not the shake.
- **World edge.** Once you are 100 nmi outside the map you see "You have left
  the theater: turn back now", repeated every 10 seconds. At 105 nmi you are lost.
  Invulnerable does not save you here. AI aircraft that stray past 105 nmi are
  removed, and nobody is credited with the kill.
- **Gear up on the ground.** Raising the gear on the runway below stall speed
  drops the aircraft onto its belly: "Gear up on the ground: belly scraping". It
  brakes hard and wears the airframe, and a long slide can destroy it. Raising
  the gear at rotation speed or in the air works as before.
- Aircraft can no longer climb far above their ceiling. Lift thins with the air,
  so a zoom climb carries you at most about 23% past it before you sink back.
- A loaded aircraft at full afterburner near its top speed no longer sinks in
  level flight.

### Smarter, safer AI pilots

- **Terrain look-ahead.** AI pilots now look about 6 seconds ahead for rising
  ground, so fast jets like the F-22 and X-31 stop flying into hills.
- **Leader succession.** When an AI wing loses its leader, the next wingman
  takes over and the others form up on it.
- **Traffic avoidance.** AI aircraft flying on their own turn away from
  predicted near misses. Mid-air collisions in the test fights fell from 15 to 2.
- **Return to base.** Once a side has shot down every enemy aircraft, its AI
  wings fly home and land, one approach at a time.
- **Same glide path as you.** AI landings fly the player's 3 degree ILS path to
  the touchdown zone, and cross the threshold about 50 to 75 ft up.
- A wingman still on the ground answers "unable" to a land order, takes off in
  turn and rejoins its leader instead of taking off only to land again.
- Airborne mission aircraft always start at least 5,000 ft above sea level and
  1,000 ft above the ground, so no enemy group starts inside a mountain.

## Improvements

### Landing

- **The ILS now guides your wheels to the touchdown zone**, 1,000 ft past the
  threshold, as the manual describes. Before, a centred glide bar put the wheels
  a few feet under the runway and short of it. The path now crosses the
  threshold 52 ft up at every airport. The glide angle stays at 3 degrees.

### HUD and cockpit

- The HUD shows **BAY** when the weapons bay is open, and the time compression
  rate (2X, 4X, 8X, 1/2X) beside the clock, as the manual describes.
- **Comma** now raises the radar and RWR range and **Period** lowers it, as the
  manual says. They were the wrong way round.
- A station that runs dry in flight stays in the WEAPONS window as a dim 0 row
  but can no longer be selected. The selection moves on to the next loaded
  station, or NAV.
- The redout and blackout veils are now drawn on the graphics card, which
  removes a stutter of about 14 ms a frame while they showed.
- Airport and place names with accented letters, such as Berezovka in the Kurile
  Islands, now show every letter everywhere they appear.

### Menus and settings

- The Quick Mission location list now offers the 16 base theaters only. The 59
  map variants were mostly incomplete, with one or two airports.
- Every menu now reads **Exit to Desktop**, including the flight menu's shortcut
  help.
- **Alt+F4** now quits from the first-run locate screen, and from the controls,
  graphics and sound screens during a flight.
- A damaged controls file no longer stops the game. It starts on the default
  controls and notes the problem in the session log. A damaged graphics file is
  also noted in the log now.
- Import errors now name the damaged or missing file and say what to do.

### Replays

- A flight started straight from the command line now records, like one started
  from the menu.
- Name labels of aircraft flying close together stack instead of printing over
  each other.
- The F-22N and F/A-XX are named correctly in recordings and Tacview files.
- The mission summary counts fuel used from external tanks as well, and counts
  airborne time only while off the ground.
- Older recordings still play.

## Fixes

- Stores taken off on the Load Ordnance page no longer appear in flight or get
  selected at start-up. An aircraft with nothing loaded starts on NAV.
- Releasing chaff or flares while an AI missile was in flight could crash the
  game. Fixed.
- A mission could end with an error when an AI aircraft dropped flares or chaff
  against some imported missiles. Fixed.
- Shooting down an enemy group that its own side had to protect failed your
  mission. Only your side's protect goals count now.
- AI wingmen no longer follow a destroyed leader in to land, or turn into each
  other when breaking out of formation together.
- AI aircraft no longer fly into rising ground while turning toward a target.
- AI wingmen under missile attack no longer flip between defending and flying
  formation many times a second, and AI fighters no longer dither between gun
  and missile attacks.
- Damaged AI aircraft returning to base no longer sink short of the runway.
- The activity line no longer keeps showing an aircraft as defending after it
  was destroyed.
- The RCS window's "no exposure data" message no longer runs over its bearing
  labels.
- With the Guns only cheat and an empty gun, start-up no longer selects a
  missile.
- The ground start hint now names key 5 for full throttle.

## Known limitations

- **Some aircraft cannot take off from the shortest strips.** On the 1,074 ft
  strips at Santa Fe, San Carlos, Goose Green and Santiago, the F/A-18D, Rafale,
  A-4E, MiG-23 and F/A-XX now need more runway than there is. AI wingmen are
  still placed there.
- **Loaded stall speeds are a rule, not measured data.** The F-22 family, Su-25,
  MiG-21 and MiG-23 still fly slower than their real counterparts, because their
  imported data is low.
- **Two AI aircraft can still collide while both defend against missiles.**
  Traffic avoidance never overrides missile defense.
- **AI aircraft with no route still fly off the map** while enemies remain, and
  are lost at the world edge.
- **The HUD is hard to read over fog and cloud.** A fix is being considered.
- **This release was tested on Linux only**, and by scripted pilots rather than
  by hand. The world edge warning has not been seen in a real window yet.
- Everything listed under [v0.1.2 known limitations](v0.1.2.md#known-limitations)
  still applies.
