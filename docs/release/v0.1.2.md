# v0.1.2: Explosions, Sound Prefs, Smarter AI and Flight Views

**Released:** 2026-09-28 · **Channel:** Pre-release · **Tag:** [v0.1.2](https://github.com/john-overton/T.O.R.E-Fighters/releases/tag/v0.1.2)

The second update to the [v0.1.1](v0.1.1.md) pre-release. Hits, kills and crashes
now use the original explosions, and crashed aircraft leave burning wrecks
behind. The original Sound/Music Prefs dialog is in, AI pilots see and shoot
better and protect their damaged aircraft, and the flight views have been
reworked from your bug reports. Fly a mission, shoot something down, and go
look at the crater.

> **Your game files are imported again on the first start.** This release needs
> more of the original's sounds and artwork, so the first launch refreshes your
> import on its own. The install steps from [v0.1.0](v0.1.0.md#getting-started)
> have not changed.

## Highlights

### Original explosions, craters and crash sites

- Every hit, kill and ground strike uses the explosion the original gave that
  weapon or aircraft, with its own size, length and sound.
- Weapon craters stay for the rest of the mission.
- An aircraft that crashes on land leaves a crater, a fire and a smoke column
  for 15 minutes. The column rises at 20 knots and fades out between 1,300 and
  1,500 ft.
- Smoke, contrails and flare smoke now drift with the mission wind.
- Sound depends on where you are. From inside a cockpit, outside sound plays at
  40% without treble. Outside, you hear your own engine from the aircraft, and
  other engines and crash fires loop from where they are, with Doppler pitch.
- Replays record explosions, craters, fires and smoke, and play them back.
  Recordings made by older builds still play.

### Sound/Music Prefs

- **Pref > Sound...** in the main menu and the paused flight menu opens the
  original SOUND/MUSIC PREFS dialog, with its knobs, levers and switch sound.
- Sliders set Overall, Engine, Weapon Lock, RWR, Stall Warn and Radio Msg
  volume, plus In-Flight and Other music, and you can swap the channels. **OK**
  saves your choices. Other music is heard while you drag its knob.
- The old placeholder Music and Effects rows and the **M** key are gone. Earlier
  settings carry over once.
- The RWR now plays the original warning tones: one for a missile in flight at
  you, and others while an enemy aircraft holds a radar or infrared lock on you.

### Smarter AI pilots

- **Better lookout.** AI pilots notice contacts they are allowed to engage and
  react to gunfire and hits even when they have no target.
- **Sustained gunfire.** AI guns fire in bursts with recovery pauses, lead the
  target from its observed motion, and shoot along the real barrel with
  ammunition counted round by round.
- **Realistic damage for every AI aircraft.** Whatever your Damage cheat, other
  aircraft take the Realistic damage model: engine, fuel, hydraulic, control and
  structural faults, lost radar, decoys and weapon stations. An AI aircraft whose
  flight ends now falls as a wreck, and the debrief credits its last attacker.
- **Damage recovery.** Damaged AI pilots stop attacking, fly home and land. They
  ease off failing engines, try to restart a flameout, limit their turns and
  speed to what the airframe can take, and eject from an uncontained fire.
- The replay debug panels explain these decisions in plain words.

## Improvements

### Flight views

Several of these came from player reports in issue #1.

- **F2** and **F3** turn about the aircraft's own axes, so the rear view moves
  the right way when you pitch or roll, and the up view no longer flips in a
  loop.
- **F2** looks from the pilot's eye and shows the airframe's spine and tails.
- Pressing **F6** again cycles through your wingmen and names each one.
- **F4**, **F7** and **F8** keep a target your sensors dropped while you can
  still see it, within 10 nmi. Without Easy targeting, it is lost for good once
  out of sight. The HUD selection and weapons still drop at once.
- **F7** draws a bearing compass at the top of the screen while it has a target.
- The fly-by view picks a new point once the subject is 3 nmi from it.
- The NAV bank scale now turns with the horizon and settles like a gyro.
- Exterior aircraft no longer shimmer.

### Weapons

- The F-22's main bays stay shut until you fire. A bay shot waits for the doors,
  fires, and the doors close a second later. **O** still opens them by hand.
- Missiles now drop about 124 ft before their motor lights, as the original's
  AIM-54, AA-9, AIM-7 and PL-10 do. AIM-54, AAM-L and PL-10 climb to 5,120 ft
  above the target and dive in from 3.3 nmi. The lob is fitted, not measured.
- Flares and chaff drift with the wind once they slow down.

### Menus and controls

- **Pref > Graphics...** in the paused flight menu now opens the Graphics
  screen.
- The controls screen has a **Find** row. Search by action name to find commands
  to bind, or by key or pressed input to see what it is already bound to. **Ctrl+F**
  jumps to it and **Esc** clears it.

### Replays

- **Esc** opens the flight pause menu in the replay viewer, with End Replay,
  Exit to Desktop, Graphics, Sound, Control, time, and switches for name labels,
  the mission timer, trails and the Comms panel.
- The replay views now match flight, including the wingman, target, missile,
  compass and zoom keys.
- A replay-only **object view** looks from any aircraft, ground object or weapon
  to any other, at any range. Press **O** or **Shift+O**, or use **View from
  here** and **Look at this** on the right-click menu.
- Replays remember what the player's target views were following.

### Import

- The import can now hold up to 32,768 resources and 1 GiB, up from 4,096 and
  256 MiB, which a full import had nearly reached. Existing imports still load.

### Linux

- Linux builds now target glibc 2.35, so the AppImage runs on older
  distributions that crashed before.
- The AppImage is now named `T.O.R.E-Fighters-<version>-x86_64.AppImage`.

## Known limitations

- **Missile lobs and drops are fitted.** The numbers match the original's flags
  and what we could measure, not a recording of the original.
- **AI pilots repair nothing.** A damaged aircraft flies home and lands, and
  wounded pilots are treated after landing, but no system is fixed.
- **The new Linux build has not been run on an older distribution by hand.** It
  is built on the release runners.
- Everything listed under [v0.1.1 known limitations](v0.1.1.md#known-limitations)
  still applies.
