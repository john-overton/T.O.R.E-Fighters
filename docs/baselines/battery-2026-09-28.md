The first overnight battery: menus, flight, AI fights and replay, all run at once
by four test lanes, with every bug found fixed on the way. How the battery works
is in [testing](../testing/README.md); each lane has its own page with the
details (bugs, known failures, decisions, things for a human eye or ear).

Branch `bug-bash` in the local repository holds everything (about 120 commits on
top of `main`). Nothing is merged into `main` and nothing is pushed.

**Final full run** on the final merged build (commit 42d181c, 16 jobs, 3 windows): 4,880 of
4,905 passed in 63 minutes of wall clock (about 15 hours of scenario time). The 25 that
were red were all understood:

- 13 `replay-combat-smoke-*` scenarios were marked as known failures that turn red
  when they start to pass, and they did, because the flight lane fixed the probe.
  The markers are removed.
- 12 AI scenarios fail on documented problems (supersonic low flight into hills,
  1,074 ft strips, a wingman far below its slot, a Key West wing leaving the map).
  They are now marked as known failures with the reason. Rerunning 17 of the
  affected scenarios after the change passed 17 of 17.

So the battery is green on the final build with 12 documented known failures, all
listed under decisions on the [AI lane page](../testing/lane-ai.md). The full battery
was not rerun a second time after those marker edits.

## What it means for the game

The player's aircraft, the menus and the recording pipeline held up well: no
crashes, no impossible flight states, no broken art in about a thousand captured
frames that were looked at by eye. The real bugs were in the details around
them and in the AI.

### Bugs fixed

**Loadout and start-up (the Maverick bug).** Taking stores off the ordnance page
left them listed, selectable and armed in flight (the ammunition count was
already right). An aircraft with nothing loaded now starts on NAV with nothing
listed, and one with only its gun starts with the gun. Checked on all 14
aircraft in a real window.

**AI.** Defects a player would notice:
- a mission ended with an error when an AI dropped chaff or flares against
  some imported missiles;
- a wingman kept landing after its leader ejected and left the fight for good;
- AI aircraft flew into hills while turning, and while facing rising ground;
- a wingman flipped between missile defense and formation flying every tick,
  and the RWR flickered with it;
- wingmen breaking out of formation turned into each other;
- a dead aircraft's activity line stayed "Defending";
- a missile threat vanished for one tick when the radar warning gave way to
  eyesight, so the AI flip-flopped;
- fighters swapped tactics 30 times in 3 seconds at the edge of a missile's
  firing zone, and between gun and missile tracking;
- shooting down an enemy group that its own side marked "must survive" failed
  the player's mission.

**Flight model.** The manual says the 1 G ceiling is where the air is too thin to
hold the aircraft up; the game kept full lift above it (an X-31 flew on at
66,700 ft against a 41,000 ft ceiling). Lift now thins above the ceiling. A
loaded aircraft on the outer band of its envelope could not hold 1 G and sank
with the autopilot engaged; the limit now never falls below 1 G inside the
envelope. Both rules are `fitted`, apply to the default hybrid adapter only, and
are described in the [flight model](../FLIGHT-MODEL.md).

**Controls and HUD (from the retail manual).** The radar and RWR range keys were
reversed (comma raises the range, period lowers it, manual pp. 21, 94, 97). The
HUD now shows BAY for aircraft with a weapons bay and the time compression rate
next to the clock, as the manual shows.

**Text.** Retail text is DOS code page 437, not UTF-8: the Kurile airport
"Berëzovka" lost its letter on the map and menus. All text drawing now goes
through the code page and `--validate-text` scans every imported string.

**Recording, replay and input.** Alt+F4 did nothing on the controls, graphics
and sound screens in flight and on the first-run screen; `--free-flight` never
recorded; the F-22N and the F/A-XX were recorded as "F-22"; replay name labels
of close aircraft overprinted each other; the mission summary called a parked
wingman airborne for the whole flight; a cockpit message with no speaker read
"someone:"; a damaged input, graphics or media file now says which file and how
to recover; about 45 bare parser messages (`invalid float literal`) now name the
option; the ground start hint pointed at a key that no longer sets throttle.

**Dev tools that had rotted.** `--combat-smoke` failed for 13 of 14 aircraft and
`--validate-creator` had failed since 2026-09-21 on stale assumptions; both are
current again. Combat tape replays drifted with no wind; fixed.

### New instruments the battery leaves behind

- `--probe-fight F:E` (up to 15 v 15), `--probe-group`, and a per-tick check for
  impossible AI states (`AI probe anomaly:`); seeded fuzzing of 400 whole
  setups (`tools/battery_scenarios/_ai_fuzz.py`).
- `--validate-creator` now also starts 11,605 Quick Mission setups, fuzzes the
  screens with about 200,000 events and renders 518 creator pictures.
- `--input-script FILE` presses keys and clicks the mouse in a windowed run;
  scripted hand-flown takeoffs, weapons, cheats, the Replays screen, the
  debrief and the pause menu all run in the battery.
- Headless flight probes with scripted pilots (spin and stall recovery,
  landing, autopilot, eject) and an `extremes:` line.

## Changes to look at first

These are behaviour changes made overnight because a document or the retail
manual said what should happen. Each is a small commit and easy to drop.

1. The flight-model envelope rules (1 G floor, ceiling lift): `fitted`, hybrid
   adapter only.
2. The radar and RWR range key swap (manual pp. 21, 94, 97).
3. The HUD BAY label and time rate.
4. AI terrain floor (levels the wings, faster pull-up), missile-threat hold,
   gun-tracking hold and escape scoring: all `fitted`, in
   [the AI spec](../spec/ai.md).
5. The CP437 menu font atlases (Noto glyphs for the accented letters; the ASCII
   cells are byte for byte unchanged).
6. `--free-flight` now records, as the docs said.

A HUD drop shadow that made the HUD readable over fog and cloud was tried and
reverted because it is a visual design choice; it is commit `78e62e1`.

## Decisions for John

Behaviour that no spec, doc or manual page defines, so nothing was changed.
Each lane page has the full list and the evidence. The ones that matter most:

- **Overspeed has no consequence** (dives to 1.5 to 1.9 times top speed; the
  manual says the wings tear off but gives no numbers).
- **The map has no edge**: aircraft fly 100,000 ft past it, with no boundary
  message. AI aircraft with no route, and wings leaving a ground start, also
  leave the map.
- **Gear can be retracted on the runway** at 80 kt and the aircraft takes off
  gear up.
- **Fast low AI flight** (supersonic F-22 family near hills) crashes because the
  source-confirmed terrain look-ahead is 1,000 ft, under a second of warning. A
  wingman far behind and below its leader in the Intercept phase cannot catch up.
- **Very short strips** (about 1,000 ft) accept any aircraft, and two mountain
  airfields spawn the enemy inside the terrain at 50 nm separation.
- **AI deconfliction outside formation**: two leaders on converging courses
  collide; nothing steers them apart.
- **Leaderless wingmen** fly straight forever; there is no succession rule.
- The Su-35 wingtip station is flagged internal by the data; the creator's
  situation and nationality rows do nothing; Enter in the creator activates the
  field where the manual says it accepts the form; the pre-flight menu still
  reads "Exit to Windows".
- A damaged `input-v1.conf` stops the game starting (documented), where other
  settings files fall back to defaults.
- Older combat tapes that cycled stations with `[` and `]` across an empty
  station will replay on a different station.

## Needs a human eye or ear

Collected on each lane page. In short: how audio sounds, the sunset transition,
night desert ground colour, white airport pavement at Athens and Stanley, cloud
and fog at the default start altitude (solid white, no ground: consistent with
the imported haze tables, not compared with retail), and the new BAY label

## Not run

Windows and macOS, real audio devices, real controllers, a hand-flown landing
(scripted pilots land; a person has not), the macOS golden values, packaging,
and any comparison with the retail game. The `--missile-acceptance` probe (320 to
480 test shots per aircraft, each checked for impossible results) takes about 40
minutes each for the F-14 and Su-35 in a debug build, so it is not in the default
battery. It was run by hand for all 14 aircraft, with no impossible rows found in the
tables that completed (the F-14 in a debug run, the rest in an optimized build; the
Su-35's debug run stopped at 171 clean rows at its time limit). To rerun it:
`TORE_BATTERY_SLOW=1 python3 tools/battery.py --scenario 'flight-slow-*' --bin target-release/release/tore-app`
with a release build.
