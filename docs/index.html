01 / The briefing

The experience.
The ambition.
The next generation.

Tasteful. Opinionated. Reverse Engineered. T.O.R.E is about recovering what it felt like to fly Fighters Anthology, then giving that experience a future.

Original aircraft data, theaters, cockpit art, menus, and sound come from your own game files. A new Rust engine brings modern controls, a steady simulation, and a foundation for much bigger things.

It is a work in progress. Faithful gameplay is the goal, with deliberate improvements and openly documented approximations along the way.

02 / Inside the rebuild

Cleared for a new era.

Already in the development build. Each system is still being refined toward the full Fighters Anthology experience.

Your aircraft. Your cockpit.

Fly the F/A-18D, Rafale C, F-14D, F-22 variants, and more. Source cockpit art, a responsive HUD, instrument windows, external views, and animated aircraft bring each sortie to life.

Explore the aircraft ↗

A familiar world below.

Explore all 16 original theaters and 59 map variants. Original terrain artwork, scenery, airports, day and night skies, clouds, fog, and moving ocean surfaces set the scene.

Explore the theaters ↗

Make every sortie yours.

Set up a Quick Mission, choose your aircraft and theater, and configure supported weapons and fuel. Try ground starts, manual weapons, radar, infrared tracking, and evolving air combat.

Read the feature matrix ↗

Fly with your own setup

Keyboard, gamepad, joystick, throttle, and pedals. Rebind controls, save profiles, and enable controller rumble.

The original atmosphere

Imported menu art and sounds, in-flight situation music, and spatial combat audio reconnect the sights and sounds.

A foundation built to last

Linux, Windows, and macOS support. A deterministic 120 Hz simulation runs independently of rendering, including headless.

04 / The flight plan

A long-range mission.

First, a faithful quick fight. Then fly together, return to the campaigns, and open the world to more. This is the broad sequence, not a release calendar.

  1. 00Foundation / ongoing

    Spec & salvage

    Recover formats, record the behavior that matters to players, and establish a portable Rust engine. Research continues as the game grows.

  2. 01In development

    Faithful quick fight

    Bring menus, terrain, flight, opponents, sensors, and weapons into a complete quick-fight loop, with installation and first-run import.

  3. 02Next milestone

    Multiplayer

    Meet in an online lobby, configure a shared Quick Mission, and fly together. Later milestones extend multiplayer to more modes.

  4. 03Planned / 1.0 target

    Missions & campaigns

    Original campaigns, objectives, pilot records, saves, carrier operations, and more aircraft. Multiplayer expands to campaigns, then single missions.

  5. 04Planned

    Tools for creators

    Aircraft and asset tools, a mission editor, sound profiles, and a mod loader. Build and share without having to write the engine.

  6. 05Planned

    The remaster layer

    Optional upgrades to menus, terrain, cockpits, lighting, and systems. Keep the classic presentation available with the extra layers switched off.

  7. 06Planned

    A persistent war

    A shared, lasting campaign with air and ground operations. Choose roles, support the wider battle, and return to a world that carries on.

  8. 07Planned

    Beyond the original maps

    Tools for custom terrain, shareable community theaters, and new aircraft and objects built through the creator tools.
