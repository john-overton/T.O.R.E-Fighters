# Reel credits

* Engine and source: T.O.R.E-Fighters, https://github.com/john-overton/T.O.R.E-Fighters.
  Captured from main `fb9c2ec`, version 0.1.3, through this isolated production branch.
* Project logos: the existing [Fighters patch](../../docs/images/tore-fighters-logo.png)
  on the end card and [aircraft icon](../../docs/images/tore-icon-fullsize.png)
  on the boot screen, reused at John's request on 2026-09-30.
* Lettering: unmodified Liberation Mono Bold, rasterized at low resolution then
  scaled with nearest-neighbour filtering. The supplied fonts are under the SIL
  Open Font License 1.1. Copyright Google Corporation (2010) and Red Hat, Inc.
  (2012). Full license: [fonts/OFL.txt](fonts/OFL.txt). The Liberation Sans
  wordmark was dropped at John's request on 2026-09-30.
* Platform marks on the end card: simple glyphs drawn for this reel to say the
  game runs on Windows, Linux and macOS, added at John's request on 2026-09-30.
  Windows is a trademark of Microsoft Corporation, Linux is a trademark of Linus
  Torvalds, and Apple and macOS are trademarks of Apple Inc. They are used only
  to name the supported platforms; no endorsement is implied.
* Gameplay art, terrain, aircraft, cockpit, HUD, replay interface, effects and
  sound: the user's own Fighters Anthology install, imported at render time and
  drawn by this engine. No retail media is in the repository. The original art and
  its visible limitations are preserved.
* Music, picked by John on 2026-09-30: two imported phrases rendered by the
  game's PCM phrase player. The briefing track `XFI204CA` is the bed, stretched
  3.2 % faster without changing pitch; `AIR003`, from the `M_SUCC.MUS` success
  score, is the ending, three bars then its own final stinger. The stretch and
  the edits between them, on the beat, are editorial. Missing music fails
  production; no replacement music is used.
* Engines, afterburner, weapons, explosions and radio: recorded cues through the
  existing game mixer, with speech kept on its own stem. For the watched AI
  cockpit's gun, the director resolves the recorded weapon's fire clip and
  starts it on each burst's first actual round. The replay blast is advanced six
  frames and allowed to ring over the paused frame, as an editorial sound edit.
* Boot Foley: the retail `&SWITCH.11K` and `&BUTTON.11K` cockpit recordings.
* Authored for this trailer, making no claim to be in-game features: the power-on,
  boot listing, static, sync wobbles, videotape treatment of the HUD camera, CRT
  finish, typography, roster panels, the badge's flip, shine and sparkle sprites,
  transitions, edit, camera paths and mix.
* Actual game interface shown as is: the replay transport, timeline, timer,
  aircraft labels and radio subtitles; the HUD, target box and gun pipper, which
  use recorded target observations and the game's existing drawing and ballistic
  solver.

Required screen credit: "Fan project. Not affiliated with Electronic Arts or
Jane's." No extracted audio, rendered frame, recording or video is committed.
